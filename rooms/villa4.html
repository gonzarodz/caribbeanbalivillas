The Story

About This Villa

Where the Caribbean sky performs its finest show.

Poised to catch the west coast's famous light, the Ocean Vista Villa is the romance of Puerto Rico distilled into one unforgettable space. This is where sunsets become appointments you keep every single evening of your stay.

A graceful queen bedroom and a spa-like bathroom — with premium bath essentials and cloud-soft towels — create a calm, comforting base. Cook in the full kitchen or order in; either way, the best seat in the house is the private balcony.

From the ocean-facing balcony, watch the sun melt into the Caribbean while breezes drift through the open doors. It's a front-row view of golden hour, night after night.
