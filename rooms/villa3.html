About This Villa

Sun, water and slow, unhurried luxury.

Bold, bright and made for long afternoons in the sun, the Pool Villa is designed around its sparkling private swimming pool. Dive in at sunrise, drift through the afternoon, and settle into golden-hour lounging on your own sun-drenched deck.

A plush queen bedroom and en-suite bath with premium essentials ensure every night ends well. The full kitchen keeps everything effortless — blended drinks, simple dinners, and snacks carried straight from the fridge to the water.

Step from the deck straight into the pool, cool down under the open-air rain shower, and finish your day wrapped in warm Caribbean breezes as the deck lights gently come on.
